PREFACE SOMNATH SEN
INTRODUCTION

Hello, I'm Somnath.

Welcome to my personal digital biography. I am an MCA student, software developer, and data analyst driven by practical engineering and empirical insights.

I do not view software development and data analytics as competing disciplines. Writing software builds the machinery; analyzing data tells us how that machinery operates and where real value lies.

Whether I am designing database schemas, architecting RESTful APIs, or cleaning raw tabular datasets in SQL and Python, I care about craft, clarity, and real-world utility over vanity metrics.

This website is structured as an interactive book — an evolving record of my journey, technical competencies, live deployments, and analytical workflows. Turn the page to continue.
